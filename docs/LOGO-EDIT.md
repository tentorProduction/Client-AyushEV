## Final image prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image 1 is the edit target: user's elegant calligraphic black capital A logo. Preserve its distinctive tall diagonal A, tapered thick right stem and sweeping crossbar. Add a small unmistakable black two-prong EV electrical plug integrated naturally into the RIGHT end of the sweeping crossbar, with the crossbar serving as the cable, a tasteful slight upward curve. Keep plug proportional and recognizable, not oversized. Remove the paper texture and ALL background completely: actual transparent alpha background, no white field, no checkerboard painted into image. Pure flat black artwork, sharp clean vector-like edges, no shadows, no gradients, no text or additional letters. Center the complete modified mark on a square transparent canvas with only about 8% clear margin, preserving all strokes and no cropped ends. This is the final logo asset for Aayush EV website and favicon; lightly refine the thinnest strokes for small-size legibility without changing the supplied identity.
